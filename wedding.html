The Wedding

AUGUST 20, 2027

Jumeirah Bali, Indonesia

We're so glad to celebrate this new chapter
with our favorite people from all over the world.
We've designed these two days for us to
relax, connect, and celebrate together.

Day one will be a welcome dinner and
Balinese festivities. Day two will be our vows,
followed by a formal dinner and dancing
under the stars.

Day One

Friday, August 20

5PMWelcome Drinks
7PMIndonesian Dinner
8PMBalinese Festivities

Venue

Akasa Jumeirah Bali

Restaurant by the beach within
Jumeirah compound.

Transportation

For those staying at Swiss-Belresort Pecatu
hotel, shuttles will depart the hotel to
Jumeirah at 4:30PM.

Shuttles from Jumeirah to Swiss-Belresort
will be at 9PM, 10PM, and 11PM.

Kids

Welcomed!

Dress Code

Tropical Cocktail

Ladies: Think upscale date-night attire for
a cliffside dinner. Please avoid red.

Gents: Collared shirt paired with long trousers
or chinos. Loafers or dress shoes. Please avoid
t-shirts, shorts, jeans pants, sneakers, flip flops
or sandals, and caps.

Day Two

Saturday, August 21

5PMCeremony
6PMCocktail
8PMReception
10PMAfterparty

Venue

Trowulan Fountain Jumeirah Bali

Dress Code

Formal / Black-Tie Optional

Ladies: Formal, elegant floor-length gowns,
vibrant colors encouraged. Please avoid
midi-length or short dresses, white or
pastel colors.

Gents: Formal dark suits (black, deep navy
or dark gray), plain white shirts, long neck tie,
black leather shoes. Please avoid light-color
suits, bow tie or casual shoes.

Transportation

Shuttles will depart from Swiss-Belresort Pecatu
at 4:30PM.

Kids

Children are welcome to celebrate with us
at the ceremony and cocktail hour. We kindly
ask that the dinner reception and after party
remains adults-only.
