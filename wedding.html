Travel & Accommodation
Flying to Bali

Fly into Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) in Denpasar; ensure your passport has at least 6 months of validity from your arrival date.

Before Flying

Check if you need a visa (here). If you do, apply for a Visitor Visa via the official Indonesia e-VOA portal.

Submit the online Electronic Customs Declaration (e-CD) within 3 days before arrival in Bali.

Pre-pay the Bali International Tourist Levy (IDR 150,000 / $8 USD) online.

Please use only the official links above.

Transportation

Airport Transfer (DPS to Uluwatu area)

The drive takes 45–60min. We recommend using Grab or Gojek for ride-hailing.

You can also arrange a private airport pick-up through your accommodation or via BlueBird Taxis. Bluebird has an official desk in the arrivals hall after customs.

Event Shuttles

For guests staying at Swiss-Belresort Pecatu, transportation will be provided to and from Jumeirah for both days. Guests staying elsewhere are responsible for their own transport.

Travel Tip

Traffic and ride-hailing wait times spike near sunset. Please allow extra travel time and book rides ~20 minutes before you need to leave.

FAQ
Are kids welcome?

Kids are welcome at all Day 1 events and the
Day 2 Ceremony and Cocktail hour.

The Dinner Reception and Afterparty on Day 2 are adults-only. Please let us know if you'd like help arranging babysitting.

Can I bring a plus one?

We are only able to accommodate guests listed on your invitation. Thank you for understanding!

Will events be indoors or outdoors?

Day 1: The Welcome Dinner at Akasa has both indoor and outdoor space.

Day 2: The ceremony, cocktail hour, and dinner reception at the Trowulan fountain area are outdoors, weather permitting. Flooring is mostly concrete with some tables on grass. Plan footwear accordingly.

What's the weather, and what to pack?

Bali has tropical weather all year-round (27–31°C / 80–88°F). Please bring breathable or light clothing, sunscreen, a hat, portable fan, insect repellent, and sunglasses.

Cards, cash, and tipping?

Visa and Mastercard work at most hotels, beach clubs, and restaurants. Carry small bills (IDR 20K–100K) for street vendors, markets, warungs (local food stalls), parking, and temple entry. Tipping isn't mandatory, but 5–10% is customary for drivers, guides, and service staff.

Getting around Bali?

• Private car + driver
Best for day trips and multi-stop sightseeing. Book via your hotel or BlueBird (metered taxi). Avoid unmarked taxis that won't use the meter.

• Grab/Gojek
Fast ride-hailing for cars or motorbike taxis.

• Scooters
Common locally but risky without experience and an International Driving Permit. Helmets are legally required.

Top places to visit?

• Uluwatu & the Bukit Peninsula (South)
Cliffs, surf breaks, sunset beach clubs (Savaya, Single Fin), Uluwatu Temple.

• Seminyak & Canggu (Southwest)
Dining, shopping, cafes, beach clubs (Potato Head, Finns).

• Ubud (Central)
Cultural and spiritual heart of Bali: Tegallalang Rice Terraces, sacred monkey forest, craft markets, sound healing, lush jungles, waterfall hikes.

• Nusa Penida & Nusa Lembongan
30–45 min speedboat from Sanur; coastal cliffs (Kelingking "T-Rex" Beach), manta ray snorkeling.

• North & East Bali (Sidemen, Amed, Munduk)
Quiet mountain views, rice fields, diving.

Cultural and religious etiquette?

• Temples
Cover shoulders and knees (sarongs usually available to rent). Don't sit higher than an officiating priest.

• On the street
Step around flower basket offerings on the ground — don't step on or over them.

• Interacting with people
Use your right hand when handing items to someone. Don't touch anyone's head, including children's. Gesture with your thumb or open hand, not your index finger.

• General
Keep swimwear to beach/pool areas, and avoid public displays of anger.

Can I drink the tap water?

No — drink filtered or bottled water only, including for brushing teeth if you have a sensitive stomach. Ice at established resorts/restaurants is made from purified water and is safe.

What power plug is used?

Type C and Type F (round two-prong, European-style), 230V/50Hz. Bring a universal adapter.

Staying connected?

An e-SIM is easiest — buy one online before you leave via Airalo or Holafly.
